Creamy Wild Mushroom Risotto

A comforting, rich Italian classic made with arborio rice, a blend of wild mushrooms, and a touch of white wine.

schedule

Time

45 mins

restaurant

Difficulty

Medium

kitchen Ingredients

  • Arborio Rice 1 cup
  • Mixed Mushrooms 250g
  • Vegetable Broth 4 cups
  • Dry White Wine 1/2 cup
  • Parmesan Cheese 1/2 cup
  • Shallot 1
  • Butter 2 tbsp

blender Cooking Steps

1

Prepare the broth

In a medium saucepan, bring the vegetable broth to a gentle simmer. Keep it warm over low heat.

2

Sauté mushrooms & aromatics

In a large, heavy-bottomed pan, melt 1 tbsp butter over medium heat. Add chopped mushrooms and sauté until browned. Remove half for garnish. Add remaining butter and finely chopped shallot, cooking until translucent.

3

Toast rice and deglaze

Add the arborio rice to the pan and stir constantly for 1-2 minutes until edges become slightly translucent. Pour in the white wine and stir until completely absorbed by the rice.

4

Gradually add broth

Begin adding the warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring frequently. Wait until the liquid is almost completely absorbed before adding the next ladle. Continue until rice is creamy but still al dente (about 20 mins).
